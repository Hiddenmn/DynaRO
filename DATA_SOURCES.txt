Author-generated resources
The eight-class annotations are project-generated and expert-reviewed; the
upstream collection did not supply this study's eight-class classifications.
Resizing, crop coordinates, class dictionary, stages and spatial splits are
recorded in reproduction/inputs. The data package also includes the semantic
and feature inputs required to reproduce the figure views.

Simulated trajectories and model outputs are generated records, not measured
future flow fields. The fixed simulator action/transition definitions, random
streams and trajectory identities are supplied with the input component.
Original saved results retain their split, seed, checkpoint and aggregation
identities. The package contains no newly trained or newly evaluated result.

Software
The numerical model definitions and preserved renderer functions come from
the project's recorded implementations. Comparison models are identified as
project adaptations, not as unmodified official repositories. Runtime
dependencies are not vendored; their own licence terms continue to apply.
No borrowed published figure image or copied layout helper is distributed.

The providers are credited for their original data. This acknowledgement does
not imply endorsement of DynaRO by the original data providers.
